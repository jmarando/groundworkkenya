# Outlook 2027 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A governor campaign gets a forward-looking section on the Strategy page, "Outlook 2027", where the team sets who is in the field and where each group of 2022 voters goes, and sees at once who leads and by how much, with plain readings whose every number is a figure. It carries the three things the user asked for on 18 October 2026: the weight of young voters and of the 2024 and 2025 protests (new registrations, their turnout and how they split, with the sourced facts beside them), the global youth push for opportunities and good governance (sourced context), and, for Nairobi, the arithmetic of a Senate bid beside a governor's race (Winnie Odinga's, announced 15 October 2026, and what it does and does not change for Babu Owino).

**Architecture:** No database change. Two small CSV files in `data/kenya` (dated, sourced context notes; the 2026 registration drive by county) are read by `src/lib/outlook-notes.ts`. A pure engine, `src/lib/outlook.ts`, takes the `Seat` of Path to City Hall (the two main candidates' 2022 votes in each constituency) and a `Plan` (a field of two to four candidates; for each group of 2022 voters the share who vote again and where those votes go) and returns each candidate's votes, county and constituency. `src/lib/outlook-youth.ts` reads the atlas's population estimates and places the county's new registrations. `src/lib/outlook-lines.ts` writes the readings with the findings engine's `Figure` and `unaccounted` check. One component, `Outlook2027.tsx`, sits under Path to City Hall.

**Tech Stack:** TanStack Start and Router, React 19, tsx test scripts. No new libraries.

**Spec:** none separate; the user's message of 18 October 2026 and the Goal, Global Constraints and Decisions below stand in. The method is that of `2026-10-17-path-to-city-hall.md`.

**Not in this plan:** the presidential Outlook (same engine, counties and the two Article 138 bars; next); polls as inputs (they are shown as context only); probabilities; saving a plan; per-constituency takes (the same takes apply in every constituency); MP and MCA outlooks (the atlas has no results by ward).

## Global Constraints

- **Places, not people.** Nothing here records, guesses or models any person's or any community's vote. A "group of voters" is the voters of a 2022 candidate in a place, or people newly on the register. The notes name no community (a test checks the shipped file). A Senate bid is treated as what it is: a separate ballot paper that cannot take a governor candidate's votes; it matters here only through which governor candidate a party backs, how many of a side's voters turn out, and how many vote the two papers differently. The screen says so.
- **2022 reproduces exactly.** The baseline plan returns the atlas's own figures: Nairobi Sakaja 699,392, Igathe 573,516, every other candidate 51,501, valid 1,324,409, constituency by constituency as Path to City Hall has them.
- **Situations are arithmetic, not forecasts.** No reading says anyone "will win"; no probabilities; presets are labelled as situations to try. Readings say what would have to be true ("needs", "would have to").
- **The model.** A `Plan` has a field of 2 to 4 names (slot 0 is the campaign's) and, for each of three groups (`yours`: the 2022 voters of the side the campaign starts from; `rival`: the other main candidate's; `new`: new registrations), `back` (percent who vote, 0 to 100) and `goes` (percent of those who vote for each of the four slots; the rest vote for someone outside the field). Votes for a slot = group votes in the place × back × goes. The other 2022 candidates' votes (county-wide only) stay with "everyone else". Values outside 0 to 100, or `goes` adding to more than 100, are clamped so no result is negative or NaN.
- **Plurality.** A governor is elected by the most votes in one round. Slot 0 "leads" when it has more than every other candidate in the field and more than everyone else together; level at the top means nobody leads and one vote settles it.
- **New registrations** are the IEBC's mass-drive figure for the county (30 March to 28 April 2026): gross, unaudited, no age breakdown published (an IEBC commissioner said on 5 May 2026 that she believes more than half were young). They are placed in constituencies in proportion to the adults the atlas's WorldPop 2020 estimate puts off the 2022 register (never below zero), because the IEBC publishes by county only. This is an assumption and the screen says so. A county with no figure, or no population estimate, shows what is missing and the group is off.
- **Every number in a reading is a figure** (`Figure`: label, value, text, source, period); `unaccounted(line)` is empty for every reading. Context notes carry their own source, date and url in the file.
- **Who sees it:** everyone who can open Strategy on a governor campaign whose county the atlas holds by constituency (today Nairobi); others see nothing new. Nothing is saved.
- **Words on screen, exactly:** "Outlook 2027", "What has changed since 2022", "The field", "Where each group of 2022 voters goes", "Votes again", "Everyone else", "New registrations", "Reset to 2022", "Looking at the Nairobi picture" (Nairobi only).
- **Tests** are tsx files in `tests/` with `eq()`/`near()`, ending with ``console.log(`${pass} passed, ${fail} failed`); process.exit(fail ? 1 : 0);``. One at a time `npx tsx --tsconfig tsconfig.json tests/<file>.test.ts`; everything `npm test`. `tsc` covers `src/` only. Pinned numbers come from an independent Python model written from these definitions.
- **Repository rules:** never run Prettier on `src/styles/groundwork.css` (add rules by hand); never name TransTally; the repository is public; commits end with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Sonnet 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`; nothing is pushed, applied or published without the user's OK.

## Decisions to confirm

1. **Nairobi governor first;** the presidential Outlook comes next on the same engine.
2. **Three groups only** (the two main 2022 candidates' voters and new registrations); the other 2022 candidates' 3.9% stay as "everyone else". The same takes apply in every constituency.
3. **Placing new registrations by unregistered adults** (the IEBC gives counties only); Langata, Starehe and Embakasi South get none because the 2020 estimate puts fewer adults there than the 2022 register holds.
4. **Presets** are three situations (2022 again; a side's voters shared between two candidates; new registrations voting as the 2022 register did), no forecasts.
5. **Notes** are dated and sourced; polls appear only as context with their limits. Check them before a candidate sees them.

## Review Focus

1. **A plan pushed past its ends** (`back` or `goes` outside 0 to 100, `goes` adding past 100, a slot outside the field): clamped, never NaN or negative. Pinned: Task 2.
2. **Level at the top:** nobody leads; `toTurn` is 1. Pinned: Task 2.
3. **A county without a population estimate or a registration figure:** the new-registration group is off and says why; allocation with all-zero weights is refused, not divided by zero. Pinned: Tasks 2 and 3.
4. **A reading that sounds like a forecast:** none contains "will win", "predict", "forecast" or "likely". Pinned: Task 4.
5. **A note that names a community, lacks a date or a source, or whose link is not https.** Pinned: Task 1.
6. **A phone:** the pool cards and the table read at 390 px with no sideways scroll. Seen in Task 5's harness steps.
