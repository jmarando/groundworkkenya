- Navigation: fixed sidebar above 1100px; grouped dismissible menu below. Page guards and menus share canOpen() in src/lib/access.ts; agents get field pages only. Why: accessible navigation and consistent access.
- Campaign isolation: campaign tables have campaign_id default my_campaign(), parent/channel-filling triggers and restrictive own-campaign policies. campaign_members holds one campaign per user; user_roles admin means super admin only. Why: enforce tenancy in the database.
- Campaign hosts show campaign-first home/sign-in; root stays general. Vite allows groundwork.ke and subdomains. Why: branding follows hostname, not stale browser state.
- Root head uses /brand/ icons and field.webmanifest on /field, site.webmanifest elsewhere. public/sw.js caches app/offline pages; IndexedDB saves only queries listed in src/lib/offline/saved.ts, never voter files or feed. The one exception is the walk lists given to the signed-in person: their doors with the names and numbers on file there, deleted when the list ends, at sign-out and after 14 days. Why: safe field use without signal.
- Channels route through unique campaign_channels kind/identifier. Email replies use <slug>+<conversationId>@in.groundwork.ke and /api/public/email/inbound with EMAIL_INBOUND_TOKEN. Why: inbound messages cannot cross campaigns.
- Listening uses Firecrawl gateway web search plus ScrapeCreators TikTok/Reddit/YouTube search; record social failures per sweep without pausing all collection. Why: survive credit outages.
- SMS uses Twilio when a number/service is configured, otherwise Africa’s Talking. /api/public/twilio/* checks TWILIO_CALLBACK_TOKEN because gateway auth hides provider signatures. Why: provider-neutral outbox.
- Home narration uses authenticated speech, streamed PCM and ordered sentence chunks, with deterministic scripts from Home data. Why: consistent natural voice without invented facts.
- Home keeps full morning story beside a stretched today/upcoming diary, secondary news full-width below. Why: at-a-glance planning.
- Individual poll invitations use campaign-scoped contacts, explicit sender permission, opt-outs, per-recipient reservations and managed email without changing consent. Inbox/polls share WhatsApp template fields and server validation; polls permit only poll templates. Why: isolation, approved wording and retry safety.
- Inbox groups filters by queue, channel/sentiment and topic; separate metadata; two-pane tablet and stacked phone views. Why: scannable messages without timestamp collisions.
- Voters uses native MapLibre pan/pinch/wheel and bounded zoom with explained ward/building actions. Zoom levels: area 8–11, constituency 11–13, ward 13–16, street 16–20. Address stores area/house/colour/base/tab; user moves write it, flights do not. Why: predictable controls and reload/Back state.
- Houses use one public/tiles/ PMTiles per campaigns.map_area (none means no map), built by scripts/geo/house-tiles.ts from Google Open Buildings v3, zoom 16, buildings layer, pc, under 25 MiB. Why: complete area read tile by tile from own host.
- Ward (MCA) campaigns: the map area is the ward alone (top "constituency"), its outline and nearest-centre demo map from scripts/geo/ward-map.ts, its houses from house-tiles.ts <cell> <id>. Demos: a campaign's own scenario where its slug has one (src/lib/demo/scenarios: roysambu, githunguri, athwana), else its office's; a demo area with no known position says offMap. The atlas holds the MCA race too (counted in the ward; atlas_elections 20xx-mca), so a ward campaign's Elections view opens on its own race, offered only on wards. Why: a ward race sees its own streets, and no invented positions.
- record_door alone writes house_visits; house_doors()/ward_houses() read colours; reads fail soft before migration. Why: one campaign-scoped retry-safe door record.
- Morning jobs use atomic leases, save dated no-source outcomes, retry only missing/zero-source automated rows hourly and retain provider pauses: a pause keeps the AI off (tried again after 6 hours) but headlines are still saved. Home shows the last week's latest story with news, saying so, until today's is in. Hourly callback catches collection failures and checks mornings before rivals. Why: no blank morning, skipped daily checks or duplicate paid work.
- Archive uses authenticated user-scoped monthly morning/diary reads. Why: historical isolation.
- Public styling stays scoped to .gw-landing with landing-specific workload/phone classes. Why: no console collisions.
- Findings are plain-code rules in src/lib/findings/ over count-only database functions ward_issues, issue_weeks, rival_mention_weeks, ward_spend, ward_supporters, house_doors; return nothing to field agents. Every narrative number is a finding figure. Why: auditable AI grounded only in counts.
- Polling centres: polling_stations holds IEBC's 2022 register (data/stations, schema 27); a position is written only by place_station, use_station_suggestion and station_here, never by a direct update. The map draws the part of the ward nearest each placed centre, worked out in the browser (src/lib/stations/areas.ts), and counts a centre's doors from the houses' plus codes. Why: IEBC publishes no station boundaries, and a position anyone could overwrite would make every number built on it unreliable.
- Walk lists: walk_lists and walk_list_doors (schema 28) are written only by create_walk_list, give_walk_list and close_walk_list; record_door alone writes house_visits, now also at a list's door with no person. A house is on at most one open list, a list is done when every door has a visit since it first went out, and the order of the doors is worked out in the browser when planning. Why: one retry-safe record of each knock, and no two people sent to the same door.
- Path to State House (Strategy): data/kenya holds IEBC's 2022 county rows (Form 34C, the diaspora kept apart) and the 13-region table; src/lib/kenya.ts reads them, src/lib/path.ts applies a region's share or turnout move and checks Article 138 (more than half of the valid votes, 25% in at least 24 of 47 counties), src/lib/path-lines.ts writes the readings with figures. Worked out in the browser: no database, nothing saved. To move a county to another region, change its region in counties.csv (npm test refuses a bad file). Regions are sets of counties, never people: nothing records or guesses anyone's community. Why: Kenyan arithmetic anyone can check, with no voter data and no targeting of communities.
- Path to City Hall (Strategy, governor campaigns): src/lib/seat.ts reads a county's 2022 governor result by constituency from the atlas the page already loads (the two main candidates' votes, the register, the county's totals; seats the figures don't separate are one bucket, the county's total less the others), src/lib/seat-path.ts applies a constituency's two-way share or turnout move and says whether the side has the most votes, src/lib/seat-lines.ts writes the readings with figures. It works for any county the atlas holds by constituency (only Nairobi today; Nyeri has the county total only, so its campaign sees one card saying so); no database, nothing saved. A constituency's "turnout" is the two main candidates' votes over the register because the atlas holds nothing more; every other candidate is held fixed. Places, never people: nothing records or guesses anyone's community. Why: the governor's own arithmetic, auditable from the atlas's rows, without inventing results by ward.
- Path to Parliament (Strategy, MP and MCA campaigns): src/lib/mp-path.ts reads the seat's 2022 result (the two main candidates), its register and each ward's register from the atlas, applies a seat-wide share move or a ward's turnout move (extra voters split as the seat did), and src/lib/mp-path-lines.ts writes the readings with figures; the race before (2017) is read when on file. Results are declared for the seat, so nothing is split by ward. No database, nothing saved. Why: an MP's arithmetic from declared results, without inventing ward results.
- Paid media, step 1 (Broadcast): media_buys (schema 29) holds each ad bought, its ward, dates and "Paid for by" line; booking one posts a pending "ads" expense through the to_ledger trigger and the amount is then fixed. Results (reach, impressions, clicks) are typed in from the platform's report until the platform reads are wired. campaigns.spending_limit_kes is the seat's gazetted limit (Gazette Notice 12251); Finance and Overview say "not on file" rather than borrow another seat's. TikTok takes no paid political ads, so it can't be booked. Why: every shilling of ad spend on the ledger against the right limit.
- Daraja Pulse (Broadcast, "Creators"): Groundwork sends a brief to Pulse's groundwork-brief and reads groundwork-report, server to server with x-job-key (PULSE_FUNCTIONS_URL, PULSE_JOB_KEY), keyed by the Groundwork campaign id; the contract is docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-20-daraja-pulse-link-design.md. The report is read without trust (src/lib/pulse.ts: only https links, malformed rows dropped). record_creator_payouts (schema 30) posts paid payouts once each as pending "creators" expenses. Candidate and managers only. Why: creators briefed and paid with disclosure, every shilling on the ledger.
- Field plan (/field-plan, schema 31): walk_lists.due_on/shift are written only by schedule_walk_list (which can also give the list); field_plan and field_plan_wards read the fortnight for the office; the field app puts today's lists first (src/lib/field-plan.ts). Stipends: stipends_from_field makes one pending stipend per person per day knocked on a walk list, approve_stipends, then record_stipend_paid (receipt by hand) or begin_stipend_payment/finish_stipend_payment (M-Pesa B2C, src/lib/mpesa*.ts, callbacks /api/public/mpesa/b2c-result|b2c-timeout with MPESA_CALLBACK_TOKEN); stipend_paid posts to Finance once. A payout with no answer stays "sending" with a note until someone checks the statement (release_stipend_payment). Agents read only their own stipends. Why: the field team planned by day, and every shilling paid once and on the ledger.
- Diary in calendars (schema 32): my_calendar_feed gives the office a private 64-hex token (made again on reset, which ends the old link); /api/public/calendar/<token>.ics serves calendar_feed_entries (service role only, 30 days back to 4 months ahead, only while the person is still on the office) as iCalendar from src/lib/calendar.ts, Nairobi time, an hour per timed entry. One-way: entries change in the Diary. Why: the diary on everyone's phone without a second place to edit it.
